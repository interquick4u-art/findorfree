Questions

  
Is this real? Who will answer me?
Yuri, the founder, reads every request and checks every answer before it's sent. You pay only after we confirm we can take the job, and if we find nothing you get 100% back.

  
How fast will I get an answer?
Level 1 usually within an hour, Level 2 in about 3 hours, Level 3 within 24 hours. We confirm your request by email within an hour.

  
What exactly do you do that Google can't?
We search in languages you don't speak, on sites and marketplaces you've never heard of, call or email when needed, and verify what we find. You get one clear answer instead of 40 tabs.

  
What if you can't find it? Refund rules
Nothing found — 100% refund. Answer delivered with sources — no refund, but if something is inaccurate we fix it for free. And before you pay, we check that the task is realistic; if not, we say so and you pay nothing.

By card (Visa, Mastercard and more) through a secure payment link we send after confirming your request. No account needed.

  
What won't you do?
We don't look for private individuals' personal data, anything illegal, or medical/legal decisions that require a licensed professional. Everything else — ask.
