Looking for something abroad? Search in the local language

Locals write their ads in their own language, on their own sites. If you search only in English or Russian, you see the small part made for foreigners.

Example: a flat in Prague. Instead of "apartment Prague", search pronájem bytu Praha. You get the listings Czech people themselves use, including ads straight from owners.

How to do it

  1. Translate your query into the local language with any translator. Keep it short: what + where.
  2. Find the local sites. Every country has its own: for flats in Czechia it is Bezrealitky and Sreality, not the international portals.
  3. Open the results with your browser's page translation turned on.
  4. Check the details before you pay or travel: full price, date, who is selling, reviews.

This works for flats, used cars, spare parts, doctors, courses, rare things: anything locals also look for.
