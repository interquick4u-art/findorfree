How much does help finding a rental abroad cost?

Moving to a new country and need a flat? There are four ways to find one. Here is what each really costs, with public prices we checked in November 2026.

WayTypical costGood to know
Search yourselfFreeTakes days or weeks. Owner ads are often only in the local language.
Local agentOften about one month's rent (it depends on the country)In Spain (since 2023) and Germany (since 2015) the landlord pays the agent for a normal long rental, not you.
Relocation serviceSpain example: about €1,450 for a full rental search, about €110 for a one-hour consultationFull support, viewings, contract help. Some services show no price at all until you call.
FindOrFreeFull project from $300, about 5 times cheaper. First find freeWe search owner listings in the local language and send a short list. Not found, no pay.

Which one to choose

  • You speak the language and have time: search yourself. Filter for private owners and never pay before a viewing.
  • You need someone to go to viewings and sign for you: a relocation service is worth its price.
  • You need good options fast, without paying a commission: ask us for a first find. It costs nothing, and you decide after you see it.

Before you pay anyone: ask for the full price in writing, ask who pays the agent's fee by local law, and check that the flat and the owner are real.

Sources: public price pages of relocation services in Spain (November 2026); Ley 12/2023 por el derecho a la vivienda, Spain (agency fee paid by the landlord); Bestellerprinzip, Germany, since 1 June 2015.
