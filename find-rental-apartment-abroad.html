What we do

  • Search local-language sites, owner-only portals and residences, not only the big English ones.
  • Filter by what really matters to you: district, budget with all charges, lift, balcony, furniture, no carpets, move-in date, lease length.
  • Check each listing: photos, real total price, deposit, who rents it (owner or agency), and what might be wrong with it.
  • Send you 3–5 options with links and honest notes. If you want, we ask the owner your questions.

A real example

A couple moving to Prague wanted a furnished 1–2 bedroom flat in the centre, with a lift and a balcony, up to 35,000 CZK, from January. Serviced residences cost 60–70k, so we searched owner listings in Prague 2 and 3 and nearby districts, checked every requirement, and found four options within a day, each with what fits and what doesn't.

How it works

  1. You describe the apartment and the city in any language.
  2. We tell you the level and the price before you pay. Relocation and apartment searches are usually a Project (from $300).
  3. We search with AI tools and check everything by hand.
  4. Not found — full refund.

We are honest: we use AI to search faster and wider, in the local language. A person checks every result before you see it. We are not a real-estate agency.
