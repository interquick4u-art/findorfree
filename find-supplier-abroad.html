Questions

How much does it cost?

We tell you the level and the price before you pay: $19, $39 or $89 depending on how hard the search is, and a Project from $300 for big searches such as a full apartment hunt.

What if you don't find it?

Then you get a full refund. That is the whole idea of FindOrFree: find, or free.

How fast will I get an answer?

A quick find takes about an hour, a search with checks about 3 hours, a deep search up to 24 hours. For a Project we tell you the time together with the price.

Do you use AI?

Yes, honestly. We search with AI tools, which lets us search faster and in the local language of any country. Every result is checked before you see it.

Is my request private?

Yes. We use your details only to do your search. We only search open sources and we don't search for private people or personal data.
