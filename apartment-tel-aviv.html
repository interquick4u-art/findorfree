Where the listings are

Israeli listings are on Yad2, Madlan and Komo, and in big Facebook groups. Most are in Hebrew, and good flats go fast.

Words you will see in the ads

  • דירה להשכרה — flat for rent
  • ממ"ד — safe room in the flat
  • ארנונה — municipal tax, paid by the tenant
  • ועד בית — monthly building fee

The real monthly price

On top of the rent, the tenant usually pays arnona (municipal tax, every two months), vaad bayit (building fee), electricity, water and gas. Arnona depends on the size of the flat and the area, so ask the owner for the last bill before you sign.

Before you sign

  • Under the Fair Rent Law, the security (deposit, guarantee or cheque) can't be more than three months' rent or a third of the total rent for the whole period, whichever is less.
  • Check who owns the flat: you can order the land registry extract (נסח טאבו) online from the Ministry of Justice for a small fee.
  • Ask if the flat has a ממ"ד (safe room) or where the nearest shelter is.
  • Never transfer money before you see the flat and sign the contract.
